# SiteWise — brick terrace study

## Seven homes / preferred concept

A continuous street-facing terrace. Seven 7 m bays on a new illustrative site.
Warm masonry, recessed outdoor rooms, pale entries and fences, dark dormered roofs.
Updated to the supplied four-bedroom plan: ground-floor kitchen/living to garden,
two bedrooms on each upper level, first-floor laundry and two rear balconies.
Building depth is now 14.4 m.

Latest architectural review: sloping front blades now divide neighbouring gardens,
loggia openings are smaller, and the adjacent windows have plain glazing.
Rear balconies have shared full-height privacy walls, double-width dormers,
flat fine perforated balustrades and timber paling garden fences.
Structure and services are retained for the next review.

The next frontage revision encloses each entry with pure-white render, a single
white door aligned toward the stair and a tall sidelight. Bins now sit along the
garden boundary, clear of garage and pedestrian approaches. The steep front roof
is retained, with a shallower pitched continuation replacing the flat top.
Both end terraces have three side windows and wider side gardens enclosed by
white picket returns on rendered block dwarf walls.

Latest detailing closes the roof joints, routes the entry electricity service
below ground, and replaces the faceted trees with branched, individually leafed
trees clear of the party boundaries. End windows are taller; the first-floor
privacy hood opens rearward and the roof-level hood opens forward. The lower
window has no hood. Downpipes are unchanged.

Terraces 2, 4 and 6 are now mirrored across their individual centre lines,
including plans, roof framing, fit-out, services, gardens and driveway crossings.
With the current numbering, garage pairs are 2–3, 4–5 and 6–7. Exposed end walls
and shared boundary elements retain their positions.

## Structure and services

The view below removes the architecture, interiors, landscape and civil surfaces
to reveal the structural and building-service geometry. Civil utility mains and
the site tank remain available in the editable model's Civil collection.
This view includes the new raked roof framing and end-window support geometry.

The updated roof framing follows all roof slopes, with raked rafters, trimmed
dormer openings, cheek/window frames and wedge-hood perimeter frames. Member
sizes are provisional; connections, loads and drainage still need coordination.
