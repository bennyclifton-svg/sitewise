# Landing terrace service audit — revised room layout

Viewer corrections use one 7 m townhouse layout, mirrored for houses 2, 4 and 6 and repeated across all seven. Original editable BIM files remain unchanged. This is a presentation inventory and connectivity review, not a construction design or clash certificate.

## Electrical

The old repeated layout had 15 lights and 9 sockets per home and missed the revised laundry/entry/dressing arrangement. The new room schedule has 16 named areas per home, each with a light and double GPO (112 of each across the terrace). It covers the garage, entry, stair hall, powder room, kitchen, living/dining area, four bedrooms, two upper wet rooms, laundry, dressing area and landings. Existing smoke alarms, switches and entry wall lights remain connected.

Room circuits use direct diagonal ceiling paths with slight curvature and local drops. The buried supply enters the front meter cabinet, then the distribution board and retained riser. Rigid protective conduit remains at the street feed and riser. Appliance circuits include oven, induction cooktop, refrigerator, washer and range hood. The rear condenser and water heater also appear in electrical as connected loads. These are the larger equipment boxes; they are retained rather than deleted as unidentified objects.

## Mechanical

Three indoor ducted units per home retain their refrigerant connections to the outdoor condenser. Six supply branches per home increase from 120 mm to 200 mm diameter. The range hood has a 200 mm exhaust route to a rear terminal. Three bathroom/powder extracts and one laundry extract per home discharge at side-facing outlets behind the main rear facade. Each of seven condensers has a rotating five-blade fan. Existing roof ventilators remain animated.

## Hydraulics

Cold/hot basin connections and basin wastes are explicit in each of the three wet rooms. WCs have cold supply and 110 mm waste connections. Both upper showers have hot/cold connections and wastes; the ground powder room retains its actual basin/WC layout without adding a shower. Each wet room has a floor waste, and the laundry has a separate floor waste plus washer supply/waste. Kitchen and laundry sink connections, water heater lines and the main stacks remain. Seven front water meters connect the street supply to each cold riser.

## Civil and landscape

Civil filtering includes gutters, downpipes, balcony drainage, front/rear pits, individual driveway detention and the 400 mm street stormwater main. Each driveway tank is 2 x 2 x 0.5 m, with its top 0.4 m below the model ground. Front/rear drainage enters the tank through the front pit; the tank overflow connects to the street stormwater main. Old direct pit-to-main bypasses and communal detention geometry are removed from this presentation. Water and sewer mains belong to hydraulic; power conduit belongs to electrical. Paving, bins and the car remain in the default scene but are excluded from isolated discipline views.

The rear boundary fence is divided by dwelling ownership so townhouse five's section is blue.

## Interaction and checks

With no clicked filter, hovering previews townhouse five only. Once a discipline is clicked, hovering another discipline previews that discipline across all seven; leaving restores the clicked discipline. Clicking the selected discipline again restores the default.

Tests cover the room fixture inventory, mirrored placement, finite generated geometry, duct diameter, condenser fan count, wet-area extracts/wastes, filter scope and street route tie-ins. Source bounds and classification corrections are generated by `export_services.py` into `terrace-services-data.json`.
