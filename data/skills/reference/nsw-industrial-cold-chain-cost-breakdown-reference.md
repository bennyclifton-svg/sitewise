# NSW Industrial Cold-Chain Cost Breakdown Reference

This reference supplies a practice-level cost structure for NSW cold stores and
food-processing facilities with material chilled, frozen or temperature-
controlled scope. It separates the base building, thermal envelope,
refrigeration, hygienic fit-out, process utilities, owner equipment and
commissioning.

The project team must establish temperatures, product flow, storage or
throughput capacity, hygiene zoning, refrigerant strategy, redundancy,
defrosting, utility loads, fire strategy and commissioning acceptance criteria
from active-project evidence. A taxonomy subclass does not establish those
requirements or demonstrate food-safety compliance.

This is structure only. It is not a rate pack, benchmark, refrigeration or
food-process design, project budget, tender recommendation, active-project
evidence or source-of-truth cost plan. Amounts, quantities, rates, percentages,
loads and programme effects must be evidenced or marked TBC.

## Workbook-Ready Groups

Use these top-level groups:

- fees, investigations and statutory charges;
- site and base-building construction;
- thermal, refrigeration, hygienic and process-support works;
- owner, operator, utility and vendor-direct packages;
- commissioning, contingency and explicit allowances.

Keep ambient building works, temperature-controlled works, process systems and
owner equipment separately identifiable. Do not hide refrigeration plant,
insulated envelope or utility augmentation within a generic services line.

## Base-Building Cost Families

| Family | Typical cost item labels and boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| Preliminaries and project controls | Site establishment, supervision, insurances, hygiene and segregation controls during works, logistics, temporary services, operational staging and security |
| Investigations, demolition and enabling works | Surveys, existing-system investigation, hazardous-materials provisions, decommissioning, demolition, temporary cold storage where evidenced, diversions and early works |
| Earthworks, civil and drainage | Cut/fill, ground improvement, roads, heavy-duty pavements, yards, dock areas, drainage, trade-waste interfaces, retention and external services |
| Substructure and slabs | Footings, slabs, freezer-floor build-up interfaces, insulation support, vapour barriers, frost-heave protection, pits, plinths, trenches and equipment foundations |
| Structure and external envelope | Concrete, precast, steel, roof, external walls, doors, canopies, loading interfaces, weatherproofing and external access systems |
| Ancillary accommodation | Ambient stores, offices, amenities, change rooms, welfare areas, control rooms, workshops, partitions, ceilings, finishes and fixed joinery |
| Base-building services | Non-process mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, fire, communications, security and controls; identify interfaces with refrigeration and process systems |
| External and site works | Parking, fencing, gates, landscaping, external lighting, washdown areas, waste areas, utility connections and authority handbacks |

| Family | Typical cost item labels and boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| Insulated thermal envelope | Insulated wall and ceiling panels, vapour seals, thermal breaks, fire-stopping interfaces, panel supports, protection and inspection |
| Cold-room doors and access systems | Sliding, rapid, personnel and fire doors, air curtains, seals, controls, safety release, dock seals and traffic protection |
| Refrigeration plant | Compressors, condensers, evaporators, vessels, pumps, refrigerant management, heat rejection, oil systems, acoustic treatment and plant-room works |

| Freezer floors and frost protection | Insulation, vapour barrier, heating or ventilation system, specialist slab interfaces, joints, protection and performance testing |
| Hygienic fit-out | Food-safe walls, ceilings, doors, kerbs, coves, resin or specialist floors, drainage interfaces, protection, sealants and cleanable details |
| Process hydraulic and washdown | Potable or process water, hot water, washdown, drainage, trade waste, pumps, treatment interfaces and testing |
| Food-processing or handling equipment | Production, packing, conveyors, racking, pallet handling and proprietary systems; show owner/vendor supply and building support separately |
| Docks and temperature interfaces | Dock levellers, shelters, seals, anterooms, staging areas, controls, traffic systems and condensation-management interfaces |

## Interface Schedule

For each refrigeration, insulated-envelope and process-equipment package,
identify the documented responsibility for:

- design temperatures, loads, diversity and redundancy;
- structure, supports, penetrations, sealing and fire stopping;
- incoming power, controls, monitoring and emergency supply;
- drainage, water, heat rejection and ventilation;
- refrigerant supply, recovery, detection and emergency response;
- hygiene-zone boundaries and temporary protection;
- freight, storage, cranage, installation and vendor supervision;
- testing media, product simulation, temperature mapping, training and
  warranties.

Carry an unresolved battery limit as a named interface row. Do not split it
implicitly between building and refrigeration allowances.

## Consultant and Tender Pricing Returnables

Requests should separately price:

- existing-condition investigations and load verification;
- concept, developed, approval and construction documentation stages;
- thermal-envelope, refrigeration, food-process, hygiene and services
  coordination;
- fire, environmental, authority and utility support where evidenced;
- vendor data review and package interfaces;
- construction-phase inspections and reporting by duration;
- testing, pull-down, mapping, integrated commissioning and handover;
- disbursements, exclusions, options and approved-variation hourly rates.

Require respondents to declare assumed temperature zones, operating profile,
product or process data, refrigerant strategy, utility data and vendor
information.

## Cost-Control Prompts

PMPs and cost plans should identify:

- the approved cost limit and building/refrigeration/process boundaries;
- operating temperatures, capacity and redundancy used as the cost-plan basis;
- energy-supply and authority augmentation responsibilities;
- live-operation, temporary-storage and shutdown assumptions;
- long-lead plant, panel and control-system procurement;
- refrigerant, testing, pull-down and performance-acceptance responsibilities;
- contingency, provisional quantities and interface-risk treatment;
- review points aligned to load confirmation, design freeze and vendor award.

All totals, contingencies, energy comparisons and option comparisons must be
calculated deterministically from approved inputs.

## Use Rules

- Treat these families as a starting taxonomy, not a thermal or food-process
  design.
- Preserve project zone, equipment-tag and vendor-package identifiers where
  they are stable and evidenced.
- Keep base building, refrigeration, process and owner/vendor packages
  separately totalled.
- Keep GST, currency, freight and utility-augmentation bases explicit.
- Prefer TBC or a documented provisional allowance to invented loads,
  quantities or rates.
- Record hygiene, food-safety and refrigerant requirements as evidence gaps
  until established by competent project sources.

## Boundary

This reference intentionally excludes:

- food-safety plans, HACCP determinations, refrigerant selection, thermal loads
  and engineering design;
- generic ambient warehouses, which have a separate governed reference;
- pharmaceutical/GMP and ISO cleanroom cost structures;
- market rates, benchmark $/m² figures, equipment prices or trade percentages;
- any instruction to use another project as evidence.
