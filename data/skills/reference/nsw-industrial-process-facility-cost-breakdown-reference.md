# NSW Industrial Process Facility Cost Breakdown Reference

This reference supplies a practice-level cost structure for NSW manufacturing,
dangerous-goods and heavy-industrial process facilities. It separates site and
building works from process equipment, process utilities, vendor packages and
operational interfaces so that an early estimate does not hide scope ownership.

The project team must establish the process basis of design, production
capacity, hazardous-area and dangerous-goods requirements, loads, utility
demands, environmental obligations, operational constraints and commissioning
strategy from active-project evidence. A taxonomy subclass is not a process
brief or regulatory classification.

This is structure only. It is not a rate pack, benchmark, process design,
project budget, tender recommendation, active-project evidence or
source-of-truth cost plan. Amounts, quantities, rates, percentages, production
assumptions and programme effects must be evidenced or marked TBC.

## Workbook-Ready Groups

Use these top-level groups:

- fees, investigations and statutory charges;
- site and base-building construction;
- process equipment and process-support systems;
- owner, operator, utility and vendor-direct packages;
- commissioning, contingency and explicit allowances.

Maintain distinct totals for building works, process works and owner/vendor
packages. State whether vendor prices include freight, duty, currency exposure,
installation, controls, testing, consumables, spares and commissioning.

## Site and Building Cost Families

| Family | Typical cost item labels and boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| Preliminaries and project controls | Site establishment, supervision, insurances, heavy-lift and logistics controls, temporary services, security, permits, interfaces and operational staging |
| Investigations, demolition and enabling works | Surveys, geotechnical and environmental investigations, hazardous-materials provisions, decommissioning, demolition, clearance, diversions and early works |
| Earthworks, civil and environmental controls | Cut/fill, ground improvement, retention, roads, heavy-duty pavements, yards, rail interfaces where evidenced, drainage, treatment, bunds and erosion controls |
| Substructure and equipment foundations | Building footings and slabs, machine bases, inertia blocks, pits, trenches, plinths, anchors, grouting, embedded items and vibration-isolation interfaces |
| Superstructure and envelope | Concrete, precast, structural steel, crane structures, platforms, stairs, roof, cladding, doors, louvres, access systems and weatherproofing |
| Internal building works | Control rooms, laboratories where non-specialist, workshops, offices, amenities, stores, partitions, ceilings, finishes and fixed joinery |
| Building mechanical services | Ventilation, comfort systems, heat rejection and smoke-control interfaces not included in a process-vendor package |
| Building electrical and communications | Incoming distribution interfaces, building power and lighting, earthing, lightning protection, communications, security and building controls |
| Building hydraulic and fire services | Domestic water, sanitary drainage, stormwater interfaces, hydrants, sprinklers, detection, warning systems, fire water and testing |
| External and ancillary structures | Pipe racks, gantries, canopies, tanks or shelters where in building scope, fencing, gates, parking, landscaping and external lighting |

## Process and Vendor Cost Families

| Family | Typical cost item labels and boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| Production equipment | Process line, machines, skids, vessels, material handling and proprietary packages; identify vendor supply, installation and integration boundaries |
| Process mechanical systems | Process piping, valves, pumps, compressed air, steam, gases, vacuum, extraction, dust collection, heating, cooling and insulation |
| Process electrical systems | Utility intake allocated to process, transformers, switchgear, motor control, variable-speed drives, process cabling, bus systems, UPS and process earthing |
| Instrumentation, controls and data | Field instruments, panels, PLC/SCADA, safety instrumented systems where evidenced, historian, network, licences, integration and cybersecurity responsibilities |
| Material handling and logistics equipment | Cranes, hoists, conveyors, silos, bins, transfer systems, docks and automation; distinguish equipment from building support works |
| Dangerous-goods and hazardous-area systems | Bunding, segregation, ventilation, detection, spill control, fire protection, hazardous-area electrical systems and emergency facilities as established by design |
| Waste, emissions and environmental systems | Trade-waste treatment, capture, filtration, abatement, monitoring, storage and disposal interfaces required by project evidence |
| Vendor logistics and installation | Design review, factory testing, packing, freight, customs, storage, cranage, assembly, installation supervision, consumables and specialist labour |
| Process commissioning and qualification | Factory and site acceptance testing, pre-commissioning, energisation, dry and wet commissioning, performance testing, training, spares and ramp-up support |
| Specialist regulated package (gap) | GMP, cleanroom, battery, waste-to-energy or other specialist regulated system—do not infer coverage; use a governed specialist reference before enabling |

## Interface Schedule

For every major vendor package, identify the documented responsibility for:

- foundations, embeds, supports, penetrations and access;
- power quality, load, controls, earthing and emergency supply;
- water, drainage, gases, ventilation, cooling and heat rejection;
- fire protection, hazardous-area controls and emergency response;
- freight, storage, cranage, installation and protection;
- software, licences, network integration and cybersecurity;
- testing media, consumables, waste, training, spares and warranties.

Unresolved battery limits should be carried as named interface rows, not spread
silently across building-services allowances.

## Consultant and Tender Pricing Returnables

Requests should separately price:

- basis-of-design verification and investigations;
- concept, developed, approval and construction documentation stages;
- process, civil, structural, building and services coordination;
- hazard, environmental, authority and utility support where evidenced;
- vendor data review and package interfaces;
- tender support and construction services stated by duration and attendance;
- factory testing, site testing, commissioning and performance verification;
- disbursements, exclusions, options and approved-variation hourly rates.

Require respondents to state the production basis, utility data, vendor
information and operational access assumed by their fee.

## Cost-Control Prompts

PMPs and cost plans should identify:

- the approved cost limit and its building/process/vendor boundaries;
- currency, freight, tax and escalation treatment for imported equipment;
- utility capacity, authority augmentation and connection responsibilities;
- shutdown, staging, temporary-production and operational constraints;
- design-freeze and vendor-data dates;
- commissioning media, waste, ramp-up and performance-test responsibilities;
- contingency, provisional quantities and interface-risk treatment;
- cost reviews aligned to process definition and vendor procurement gates.

All totals, currency conversions, contingencies and comparisons must be
calculated deterministically from approved inputs.

## Use Rules

- Treat these families as a starting taxonomy, not a process design.
- Preserve project work-breakdown, equipment-tag and vendor-package identifiers
  where they are stable and evidenced.
- Keep building, process and owner/vendor packages separately totalled.
- Keep GST, currency and freight bases explicit.
- Prefer TBC or a documented provisional allowance to invented loads, quantities
  or rates.
- Record process safety and regulatory requirements as evidence gaps until
  established by competent project sources.

## Boundary

This reference intentionally excludes:

- pharmaceutical/GMP, cleanroom, battery-manufacturing and waste-to-energy
  specialist cost structures;
- cold stores and food-processing cold-chain systems, which have a separate
  governed reference;
- process design, production capacity, hazardous-area classification or
  regulatory conclusions;
- market rates, benchmark $/m² figures, equipment prices or trade percentages;
- any instruction to use another project as evidence.
