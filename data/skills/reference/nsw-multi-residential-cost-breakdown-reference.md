# NSW Multi-Residential Cost Breakdown Reference

This reference supplies a practice-level cost structure for NSW apartment,
Build-to-Rent, student-housing and applicable social/affordable-housing
projects. It is intended for multi-storey or common-property residential
developments whose cost structure is materially different from a detached
house, townhouse or domestic alteration.

The project team must confirm the NCC classification, tenure model, operator
requirements, unit mix, accessibility obligations and approval pathway. A
taxonomy subclass does not establish any of those facts. Student housing and
social/affordable housing may need additional operator, funding, Class 3,
accessible-housing or government-procurement packages that this reference only
flags.

This is structure only. It is not a rate pack, benchmark, project budget,
tender recommendation, active-project evidence or source-of-truth cost plan.
Amounts, quantities, rates, percentages, dates and scope allocations must come
from active-project evidence or remain clearly marked TBC.

## Workbook-Ready Groups

Keep the cost plan in these top-level groups:

- fees, levies and statutory charges;
- consultants and investigations;
- construction works;
- client-direct, operator and authority works;
- contingency and explicit allowances.

Do not bury land, finance, sales, leasing, operating, funding-compliance or
developer overhead costs in the construction subtotal. Show them separately
only when the approved project brief requires a development-cost view.

## Construction Cost Families

| Family | Typical cost item labels and boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| Preliminaries and project controls | Site establishment, supervision, insurances, cranes and hoists, amenities, temporary services, safety systems, neighbour controls, traffic management and project-specific staging |
| Investigations, demolition and enabling works | Surveys, investigations, hazardous-materials provisions, demolition, protection, utility diversions, temporary works and early works; distinguish consultant investigations from physical works |
| Earthworks, retention and ground treatment | Bulk excavation, spoil handling, shoring, dewatering, contamination response, ground improvement, retention and under-slab treatment |
| Substructure and basement | Piling, footings, pile caps, basement slabs and walls, tanking, subsoil drainage, ramps and basement ventilation builder's work |
| Superstructure | Concrete, reinforcement, post-tensioning, precast, structural steel, loadbearing systems, stairs and structural temporary works |
| Façade, windows and external doors | Curtain wall, windows, doors, balustrades, masonry, cladding, façade access provisions, sealants, flashings and interface fire stopping |
| Roofing and waterproofing | Roof build-up, membranes, overflows, podium and balcony waterproofing, planter boxes, wet-area systems and testing |
| Vertical transport | Passenger, goods, accessible and fire-isolated lift packages; lift finishes, controls, builder's work, testing and authority interfaces |
| Apartment or room fit-out | Partitions, linings, doors, hardware, ceilings, joinery, kitchens, wardrobes, finishes, sanitary fixtures and fixed appliances; preserve unit-type or room-type distinctions where evidence supports them |
| Common-area fit-out | Entries, lobbies, corridors, stairs, mail areas, resident amenities, management areas, back-of-house rooms, common sanitary facilities and wayfinding |
| Mechanical services | Ventilation, air conditioning where specified, car-park systems, stair pressurisation, smoke-control interfaces, controls, metering and commissioning |
| Electrical services | Incoming supply, substations where required, main and tenancy distribution, lighting, emergency lighting, generation or storage where specified, metering, earthing and testing |
| Hydraulic services | Water, sanitary drainage, stormwater, gas where applicable, hot water, pumps, tanks, trade waste where applicable, metering and authority connections |
| Fire and life-safety services | Hydrants, hose reels, sprinklers, detection, warning and intercommunication systems, smoke-control interfaces, fire stopping, signage, testing and certification |
| Communications, security and resident systems | Carrier pathways, structured cabling, intercom, access control, CCTV, resident communication, operational technology and system-integration allowances |
| External works and public domain | Roads, parking, pavements, drainage, landscaping, fencing, lighting, utility connections, public-domain works and authority handbacks |
| Testing, commissioning and handover | Integrated testing, balancing, witness testing, training, as-builts, O&M manuals, asset registers, warranties, defects close-out and occupation-stage support |
| Specialist or tenure package (gap) | BTR operator systems, student-housing management systems, social-housing requirements, commercial tenancies, communal catering, specialist accessibility or other operator packages—separate and define from project evidence |

## Unit, Common-Property and Operator Splits

Where the project evidence supports it, provide identifiable subgroups for:

- apartment or room types and upgrade options;
- common property and shared amenities;
- retail or commercial components;
- operator-supplied furniture, equipment and technology;
- utility and authority works;
- display units, leasing areas and temporary occupation facilities;
- affordable, accessible or adaptable dwelling requirements.

Do not use a blended cost family to conceal different specifications, tax
treatments, approval obligations or procurement routes.

## Consultant and Tender Pricing Returnables

Consultant and contractor requests should require comparable pricing for:

- investigations and enabling works;
- concept, design development, approval and construction documentation stages;
- authority, utility and certifier coordination;
- unit-type documentation and design-change assumptions;
- façade, waterproofing, fire, vertical-transport and building-services
  coordination;
- construction-phase services stated by duration, meeting frequency, inspection
  allowance and deliverable;
- commissioning, occupation, handover and defects services;
- disbursements, exclusions, options and approved-variation hourly rates.

State whether staged approvals, staged occupation, early works, display units
or operator reviews are included. Do not infer them from the building subclass.

## Cost-Control Prompts

For PMPs and cost plans, identify:

- the approved cost limit and GST basis;
- whether land, finance, marketing, loose FF&E, operator technology and utility
  upgrades sit inside or outside that limit;
- the unit mix, common-area scope and specification baseline used by the plan;
- design-freeze gates for structure, façade, unit types and services;
- basement, authority, latent-condition and escalation positions;
- the treatment of provisional sums, contingency and client change;
- review points at concept, design development, pre-tender, tender
  recommendation and post-contract forecast.

All totals, contingencies, deltas and percentages must be calculated by the
workflow's deterministic arithmetic, never copied from this reference.

## Use Rules

- Use these families as a starting taxonomy, not a mandatory schedule.
- Preserve project-specific QS, tender or contract labels when they provide
  better granularity.
- Keep exact approved cost-item text stable for later invoice, variation and
  workbook reconciliation.
- Keep GST basis and inclusion boundaries explicit.
- Prefer a lump-sum TBC allowance over unsupported quantities or rates.
- Mark an unresolved owner, operator, authority or contractor interface as an
  interface; do not assign it silently.

## Boundary

This reference intentionally excludes:

- detached houses, domestic renovations and low-rise townhouse cost structures;
- residential aged-care and retirement-living specialist care or operator
  requirements unless a governed project-specific overlay supplies them;
- market rates, benchmark $/m² figures, trade percentages or supplier pricing;
- planning, code, funding or accessibility conclusions not established by
  project evidence;
- any instruction to use another project as evidence.
