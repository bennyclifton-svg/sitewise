Stage 1 is billed on completion. Stage 2 and Stage 3 may each be billed in two equal progress claims. Invoices are due 14 days from invoice date.

## Exclusions and disbursements

Consultant fees, authority fees, planning portal charges, survey costs, printing, travel outside metropolitan Sydney and specialist reports are excluded. Approved authority charges are reimbursed at cost and separately identified on an invoice; their GST treatment follows the underlying supplier receipt.
