## What the comparison should surface

| Discipline | The exclusion that decided it |
|---|---|
| Architectural | Kestrel excludes the Heritage Impact Statement, assuming the planner covers it. Loftus — the cheap planner — excludes it too. **Priced separately, neither carries it.** |
| Town Planning | Loftus excludes the Heritage Impact Statement. In a conservation area it is the single most important document in the application. |
| Structural | Grimshaw Vale excludes footing investigation **and** underpinning design. The owner's brief names unknown footings as a specific worry. |
| Civil / Stormwater | Stormline excludes detailed OSD design and the compliance certificate. Inner West requires both. |
| Certification | Pinnacle excludes the Occupation Certificate ($890) and pool barrier certification. Added back, it lands within $300 of Meridian with fewer inspections. |

**The heritage gap is the one worth holding on screen.** Take the two cheapest proposals —
Kestrel at $68,500 and Loftus at $7,800 — and the Heritage Impact Statement is excluded by
both. A comparison that reads each proposal in isolation misses it. A comparison that reads
them against each other finds a document nobody has priced.

## The check

If the comparison ranks purely on fee and recommends the five cheapest, that is the defect
this step exists to catch. Total on that basis is $92,300 — an apparent saving of $21,500
that buys an unpriced heritage statement, an unpriced underpinning design, an unpriced OSD
design and an unpriced Occupation Certificate.
