## Corrected for scope

Southbrook's exclusion clause — *"anything not shown on the drawings supplied… items
subject to client selection… specialist works and trades outside our normal scope"* — is
broad enough to carry five significant omissions. None of them are stated as exclusions;
they are simply absent from the six lines of pricing.

| Omission | Basis for the adjustment | Southbrook | Halden |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Plunge pool, plant and barrier | Not in any of the six lines. "Specialist works outside our normal scope." | +$78,000 | included |
| OSD tank, stormwater and kerb connection | External works at $66,000 cannot carry deck, paving, fencing **and** a 4.2 m³ detention tank | +$24,500 | included |
| Scaffolding, hoarding and site protection | No preliminaries line at all | +$18,400 | included |
| PC sums — floor coverings, appliances, tapware, light fittings | "Items subject to client selection" excluded, and no PC sums carried | +$62,000 | partly |
| Heritage-matched face brickwork to street elevation | DA condition 11. Priced by both others as a separate line | +$14,800 | included |
| Appliances | Stated exclusion — Halden is explicit about it | — | +$18,000 |
| Soft landscaping and planting | Stated exclusion — Halden is explicit about it | — | +$13,200 |

## The distinction that matters

Halden and Southbrook both exclude things. The difference is that **Halden says so.**

> *"Kitchen and laundry appliances. We have priced the joinery to receive them but have not
> allowed for supply. Allow approximately $18,000."*

That is a builder handing the client a number they can act on. Southbrook's omissions are
carried by a general exclusion clause and are only findable by pricing the scope
independently and noticing what is missing.

A comparison that only ranks submitted prices puts Southbrook first. A comparison that
checks each tender against the scope puts them last.

## Outcome

**Halden Building Co appointed 14 November 2025.** Contract sum **$748,900 excl GST**
against the owner's $750,000 construction budget, with the appliance and landscaping
exclusions carried into the cost plan as separately funded items.

Kingsford Bay's submission was the most thorough of the three and their qualifications on
underpinning remeasurement and brick supply were both sound. On corrected price they were
$12,300 behind.
