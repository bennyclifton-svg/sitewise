## Cost plan summary and control decision

This is an early operational cost plan for PM review, structured under Fees and charges, Consultants, Construction, and Contingency / allowances. All figures in the tables are **ex GST**. Owners may commonly think in GST-inclusive amounts; any owner affordability test should therefore translate the approved ex-GST control figure to an inc-GST view once the GST treatment and recoverability position are confirmed.

No approved budget, fee proposal, builder price, contract, claim, schedule of values, or variation record has been supplied. Every project-specific amount below is consequently an **Assumption** or TBC, not an approved budget. The industrial warehouse reference provides structure only and expressly does not provide market rates. The fire-services scope, GFA, operational constraints, approval pathway, procurement route, and existing-building condition remain unconfirmed.

The adopted construction reference is **TBC — Assumption**, to be replaced by an evidence-backed scope and price basis before procurement. Major fee treatment is shown separately rather than hidden in construction. Construction contingency is also shown separately and must be calculated on construction cost only after scope, access, live-environment, services-interface, and authority risks are assessed; it must not silently absorb unresolved scope.

**Control decision:** do not approve or represent a locked project budget at this stage. The recommendation is to obtain a measured fire-services brief, existing-services information, approval-pathway advice, and comparable trade pricing, then issue Cost Plan v2 with a nominated ex-GST control reference before tender or contract commitment.

- **Allowances:** Planning, certifier, consultant, investigation, construction contingency, testing, commissioning, and reinstatement allowances remain TBC. No allowance is approved by inclusion in this draft.
- **Owner-supplied items:** Any owner-supplied panels, equipment, access systems, operational shutdown resources, IT/network interfaces, or specialist equipment are excluded until identified, assigned a responsible party, and priced.
- **Open exclusions:** GFA and warehouse geometry; fire-system asset register; hydraulic capacity; pump/tank condition; electrical supply; hazardous processes or dangerous goods; access, staging, shutdowns, night work, temporary protection; demolition, asbestos or hazardous materials; structural builders-work; authority upgrades; racking, dock automation, conveyors, and other specialist systems.
- **Role boundary:** The architect-PM is treated as a client-side advisor for this draft, not the builder, Certifier, Superintendent, or contract administrator unless separately appointed in writing. PI insurance currency and scope must be checked before reliance on procurement or contract-administration services.
- **Statutory checks:** Applicability of HBCF/HOW, builder licence, LSL receipt, contract works insurance, and other NSW requirements must be verified against the actual class, contract and statutory trigger. Residential BASIX controls are not assumed to apply to this industrial refurbishment. Sydney Water and NSW Planning Portal interfaces are to be checked where the scope affects those authorities.
