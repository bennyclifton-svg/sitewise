# Project Cost Plan

## Cost plan summary and control decision

**Project:** Test-Mech-Plant  
**Profile:** NSW commercial office refurbishment; early brief-planning stage; traditional procurement; unrestricted access, vacant operation, clean/non-contaminated and non-flood-prone context are treated as planning assumptions pending project evidence.  
**Currency basis:** All cost-plan tables are **ex GST**. Commercial owners may think in inc GST terms; once the cost-control reference is populated, the owner should request an inc GST translation for cash-flow and funding decisions. No approved budget is established by this draft.

The plan is a whole-project operational control view covering fees and statutory charges, consultants, tenant construction works, client-direct or landlord interfaces, and contingency / allowances. The construction structure follows the NSW commercial fit-out reference rather than a single construction-contract line. No project cost evidence, fee proposal, contract, claim, schedule of values or variation record is available, so every project-specific amount is an **Assumption** and remains TBC.

The adopted construction reference is the detailed Construction subtotal in the table below, to be populated from an existing-condition survey, scoped design information and competitive returnable pricing. The major fee treatment is to keep planning, certifier and consultant costs outside Construction so that PM / architect fees are visible and not absorbed into the builder allowance. Construction contingency is a separate control reserve and must not absorb unresolved scope without an explicit scope decision. **Recommendation:** do not approve procurement or present this plan as an affordable budget until the owner stated budget, measured scope, consultant fee proposals and builder pricing are reconciled.

Control decision: proceed only to evidence collection and scope definition, with the cost-control reference held as TBC pending a priced brief and at least one reliable construction breakdown.

## Commitments, allowances and exclusions

No locked appointments, contracts or commitments are evidenced; therefore no supplier amount is shown as committed.

- **PC sums:** carry separately for owner-selected joinery, flooring, lighting, appliances, sanitaryware and other nominated items. They are allowances, not approved selections or fixed contract values.
- **Provisional sums:** reserve only for genuinely undefined work such as existing services, hazardous materials, fire interfaces, slab penetrations or authority connections. Each must have a scope, trigger and approval path.
- **Owner-supplied items:** loose furniture and workstations; IT hardware, software and carrier services; AV equipment; artwork; loose equipment; relocation and occupation costs. Confirm whether installation is by the builder or owner.
- **Client-direct / landlord interfaces:** base-building works, landlord review requirements, make-good obligations, utility upgrades and access charges remain outside tenant Construction until allocation is evidenced.
- **Exclusions:** finance, legal costs, tax advice, rent or holding costs, business interruption, escalation, latent conditions and statutory charges not yet identified are excluded or TBC; none is an approved allowance.
- The architect-PM fee must remain visible in Consultants. The architect-PM is advisory and is not the builder, Certifier, Superintendent or contract administrator unless separately appointed in writing. PI insurance currency and scope must be confirmed before reliance on procurement or contract-administration services.

## Source evidence and audit trail

- **Evidence map:** No active-project cost evidence was supplied. The project context identifies a NSW commercial office refurbishment at brief-planning stage with traditional procurement and CDC selected in the context lens, but those selections are not treated as project evidence in this platform-seeded draft. The cost structure is based on the mandatory NSW commercial fit-out reference and the cost-management and architect-PM role seeds.
- **Facts:** The mandatory source set defines the commercial fit-out workbook groups, tenant construction families, client-direct / landlord interfaces, and the rule that the reference is structure only and not a market-rate source. The cost-management seed supports visible fees, PC sums, provisional sums, contingency, GST treatment and claim discipline. The role seed supports separate architect-PM and owner briefs, role boundaries, builder evidence verification and owner escalation.
- **Assumptions:** All amounts, quantities, appointments, existing conditions, scope labels, approval applicability, landlord requirements, programme, area and affordability are Assumptions or TBC. No row is an approved budget, commitment or contract sum. The contingency band is an early-stage placeholder and must be recalibrated after scope evidence.
- **Judgements:** A detailed commercial refurbishment work-package structure is more useful than a single construction line. Fees and PM treatment remain outside Construction. The cost-control reference is qualified until scope and pricing are reconciled.
- **Recommendations:** Collect the evidence listed under Next actions, then replace TBC rows with deterministic ex-GST values, identify GST-inclusive cash requirements, and issue a revised cost plan before procurement.
- **Cost evidence conflicts:** None identified because no progress claim, payment claim, schedule of values, trade schedule, contract sum or variation record was supplied.
