# Project Cost Plan

## Cost plan summary and control decision

**Project:** 1.3 Mechanical plant replacement  
**Profile:** NSW commercial office refurbishment, brief-planning phase, with mechanical HVAC replacement and existing-building-condition risk identified.  
**Basis:** All monetary figures in the tables are **ex GST**. The current planning signal of around $180,000 is not project cost evidence in this draft mode and is therefore treated as an **Assumption**, not an approved budget. Owners who think in inc-GST terms should allow for GST separately when testing cash funding and approvals.

No appointment, tender return, contract sum, claim, schedule of values, fee proposal, site address, programme, quantity, plant schedule or existing-services survey is available. The plan is consequently a controlled workbook scaffold rather than an approved estimate. The adopted construction reference is **TBC**, pending a defined plant replacement scope and a returnable trade/work-package price. Fees are shown separately from construction so that PM treatment is visible and is not absorbed into a contractor allowance. A construction contingency is retained as a separate line and must not be used to conceal unresolved scope.

**Control decision:** Do not approve procurement or treat the approximately $180,000 signal as a locked budget until the owner confirms the procurement route, operational constraints, plant duty and replacement boundaries, and the project team obtains an existing-condition/services survey and comparable mechanical pricing. My recommendation is to issue a short scope-and-price request after those gates are resolved, then reset this plan against the returned work-package breakdown.

## Commitments, allowances and exclusions

No locked appointment, contract, purchase order or payment commitment is evidenced; therefore no material commitment table is populated. This protects the plan from inventing supplier, amount or approval status.

- **PC sums:** None established. Plant, controls, access equipment and specialist components must be priced from a schedule rather than treated as an unbounded PC sum.
- **Contingency:** A separate early-stage allowance is required for concealed services, access, shutdowns, hazardous materials, structural supports, commissioning rework and scope development. Release requires recorded owner approval.
- **Owner-supplied items:** None identified. Potential owner-direct items include tenant decanting, temporary accommodation, loose equipment, IT changes, energy-monitoring subscriptions and relocation costs; ownership and budget treatment are TBC.
- **Open exclusions:** Site access, lifting and cranage, after-hours work, temporary cooling, outage costs, base-building approvals, landlord works, asbestos or other hazardous-material remediation, fire engineering, structural strengthening, utility authority charges, BASIX applicability, and post-handover maintenance are all excluded from the current priced total until confirmed.
- The architect-PM remains an advisor, not the builder, certifier or Superintendent unless separately appointed in writing. Fee scope, PM fee treatment, authority to assess claims, and PI insurance currency and scope must be recorded before procurement or contract-administration reliance.
