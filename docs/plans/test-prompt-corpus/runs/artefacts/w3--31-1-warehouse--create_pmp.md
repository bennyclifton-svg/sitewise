## Cost Planning
<!-- clerk:block id=blk_4530c7ea994ade3a8012827308a4211c -->
The $18m figure is an **Assumption** as to inclusions, GST treatment, escalation date, contingency and whether it includes consultants, approvals, utilities, external works or tenant works. It is not a cost plan or tender estimate. Establish a warehouse cost plan structured around preliminaries, site establishment, earthworks, substructure, structure, envelope, roofing, docks, services, office fit-out, external works, authority contributions and contingency.
Cost risk is concentrated in unknown ground conditions, hardstand and drainage extent, utility augmentation, fire-services strategy, operational equipment, programme duration and procurement market response. Allocate design-development allowances rather than treating unknown scope as fixed. Maintain a change log distinguishing owner change, design coordination, authority condition, latent condition and contractor proposal. Report approved budget, committed cost, forecast final cost, contingency drawdown, provisional allowances and exclusions at each stage.
Recommendation: commission an elemental cost plan at concept completion, test it at design development, and require a tender reconciliation before award. Do not benchmark this project using the seed taxonomy as a market-rate source.
