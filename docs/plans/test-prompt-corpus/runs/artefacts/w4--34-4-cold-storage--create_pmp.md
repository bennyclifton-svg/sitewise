## Brief
<!-- clerk:block id=blk_b979633310e95cebcca46b26f9b078a2 -->
The physical brief is a new 8,000 m² industrial cold-storage facility (Class 7b) within a $45m stated budget. It includes external drainage, stormwater management, internal roads/pavements, mechanical/HVAC, electrical power, hydraulic/plumbing, fire services and BMS controls. The design must establish chilled, frozen and ambient areas; storage capacity; loading and traffic interfaces; thermal-envelope performance; refrigeration concept; utility demands; and operational acceptance criteria. These items are **Assumption** pending the owner brief and specialist advice.
Fire-services scope is to define passive and active measures, fire-water supply, hydrants, pumpsets, detection/alarm interfaces, access for emergency services, testing and handover records. Interfaces include civil levels and drainage, traffic circulation, electrical capacity and resilience, hydraulic/fire-water capacity, mechanical plant, BMS alarms and owner operational equipment. No exclusion is evidenced.

## Cost Planning
<!-- clerk:block id=blk_b7a881756cf0372aac3b6bd2de371cda -->
The stated budget is $45m; its inclusions, escalation date, GST treatment, contingency, consultant fees, authority fees, utility works, owner equipment and financing assumptions are **Not evidenced**. The cost plan must separate base building, thermal envelope, refrigeration, hygienic fit-out, process utilities, owner equipment and commissioning so that cold-chain decisions are visible rather than absorbed into a general building allowance.
Recommendation: commission an elemental cost plan after the physical brief and services concept are coordinated, then reforecast at each design freeze and before tender. Carry identified allowances and risk contingency separately from contractor preliminaries and provisional items. No market rate, benchmark, contingency percentage or procurement saving is asserted in this PMP.
Cost controls must identify power connection/resilience, water/fire-water infrastructure, roads and drainage, specialist plant, BMS integration, commissioning, testing, operational training and long-lead procurement. The project lead should maintain an approved budget, committed-cost forecast, change register and variation log once procurement documentation exists. Any scope change affecting cold-room capacity, temperature regime, plant redundancy, fire-water infrastructure or utility demand is a cost-and-programme gate, not a late documentation adjustment.
