Sitewise, in light

The identity is built for dark, low-glare interfaces. Every surface in this system is described by how it receives light — not by the colour it is painted.

01
Logo

The lockup pairs the isometric cube with the lowercase wordmark. The cube's three facets are the origin of the whole palette: shadow, mid, and lit face.

Clear space

Free space on all sides equal to the cube's height. Nothing crosses it.

Minimum size

120px on screen, 32mm in print. Below that, the cube stands alone.

Placement

Always in the lit half of a composition. Never in a falloff gradient.

05
Light

One key light, positioned above and to the left, roughly 20° off the surface normal. Elevation is expressed as luminance, never as a drop shadow. Move your cursor — the key follows.

Falloff
Intensity decays smoothly across a surface. No hard terminator, no banding — always blur ≥ 30px.
Contact
Surfaces darken where they meet. A tight, opaque occlusion — not a soft glow offset downward.
Bounce
Saturated surfaces bleed their hue onto neighbours at ≤ 8% opacity. Blue bounces; grey does not.
Specular edge
Only the edge facing the key catches highlight. Top border bright, bottom border black.
