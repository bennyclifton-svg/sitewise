Brand Guidelines — v1.0

Sitewise visual identity

The core rules for applying the Sitewise mark, palette and type. Follow these when producing product, marketing and partner material.

01 — Logo

The primary lockup pairs the isometric cube mark with the lowercase wordmark. Use it on black wherever possible.

Clear space

Keep free space equal to the height of the cube on all four sides. Nothing crosses that boundary.

Minimum size

Full lockup: 120px wide on screen, 32mm in print. Below that, use the cube mark alone.

02 — Misuse
Do not rotate or tilt the lockup.
Do not recolor the mark.
Do not stretch or condense.
03 — Color

Black carries the identity. Blue is the single accent, used for emphasis and interactive states. Greys handle structure and secondary text.
