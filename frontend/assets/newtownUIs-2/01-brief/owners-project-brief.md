# Owner's Project Brief

**Project** 41 Georgina Street, Newtown NSW 2042 — rear extension and first-floor addition
**Prepared by** Daniel and Anthea Marchetti
**Date** 18 March 2026
**Revision** A — issued to project manager and design team

---

## 1. Purpose of this brief

We bought No. 41 in 2019 and have outgrown it. We have two children (9 and 12) and Anthea's
parents stay with us four or five weeks a year. We want to stay in Newtown, so we are
extending rather than moving.

This brief sets out what we want, what we have already decided, and what we are still open
on. Where we have not made up our minds we have said so rather than guessing.

---

## 2. The property as it stands

Single-storey semi-detached cottage, c. 1908, attached to No. 43 along the eastern boundary.
Face brick with a rendered front, corrugated steel roof, original front two rooms with
fireplaces and ceiling roses. The rear third of the house — kitchen, bathroom, laundry — was
added at some point in the 1980s and is poorly built. There is a non-original rear verandah.

- Site area **232 m²**, frontage **6.4 m**, depth **36.2 m**
- Existing gross floor area **88 m²**
- 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom
- No off-street parking; we do not want any
- Site falls approximately 900 mm from the rear boundary to the street

We are inside the Newtown/Enmore Heritage Conservation Area. The house is not individually
listed. Our neighbours at No. 43 did a similar first-floor addition in 2021.

---

## 3. What we want

**Upstairs** — a master bedroom with an ensuite and a walk-in robe, a separate sitting room
we are calling the parents' retreat, one more bedroom, a second bathroom, and a small study
nook off the landing for the kids' homework. The retreat is genuinely for Anthea's parents
when they visit, and a second living space the rest of the year.

**Downstairs** — take out the whole 1980s rear section and replace it with a new kitchen
opening onto a single open-plan living and dining space, with a glazed rear wall out to a
covered deck. Keep the two front rooms as bedrooms and keep the original hallway and its
arch. New laundry with a door to the side passage, and a powder room under the new stair.

**Outside** — a covered deck off the living space, a landscaped rear courtyard, and a plunge
pool. The side passage stays as it is for access and bins.

## 5. Fixtures, fittings and equipment

Where we have landed on something, it is below. Where we have not, we have said what we
care about instead. We would rather the architect argue with us now than after ordering.

### Kitchen

We looked at the Milette range at the Alexandria showroom and want to specify it. We want
the 760 mm pyrolytic oven, a combi microwave stacked above it, a 900 mm four-zone induction
cooktop, and an undermount rangehood so nothing sticks out over the island. Induction, not
gas — we are taking the gas out of the house entirely.

- Benchtop: honed stone, not engineered quartz. Open on brand.
- Splashback: full-height tile, not glass.
- Cabinetry: matt two-pack, colour undecided.
- Integrated dishwasher and fridge. Fridge is existing and moving with us.

### Bathrooms

- Ensuite: double vanity, walk-in shower, wall-hung pan, no bath.
- First-floor bathroom: freestanding bath, separate shower.
- Powder room: wall-hung basin, smallest available pan.
- Tapware and fittings: brushed brass throughout. We have not chosen a range.
- Floor tiles: we want the same tile in all three wet areas.

### Lighting

Please specify from the Lumenaire catalogue we were given. We want the adjustable spots on
the ground floor rather than fixed downlights, LED strip under the kitchen overheads and in
the stair, wall lights either side of the bed in the master, and an external light at the
deck and the side passage door. We do not want downlights in the bedrooms.

### Heating and cooling

Ducted reverse cycle throughout, zoned so the upstairs and downstairs run separately. We
were quoted on a Kuroda system by the mechanical contractor who did our neighbour's house
and want that priced. Condenser needs to sit where it will not annoy No. 43.

### Floors

- Retained rooms and hallway: existing boards sanded and re-finished.
- New ground floor: engineered oak, wide board.
- Upstairs: carpet in bedrooms and retreat, tile in wet areas.
- Deck: hardwood, oiled not stained.

### Windows and doors

Timber-framed to the street elevation for heritage reasons. Aluminium acceptable to the rear
where it is not visible. Rear wall to be a full-width sliding or bi-fold — we do not mind
which, but we want the opening as wide as the structure allows.

## 6. Budget

We have **$750,000** for construction. We understand consultant fees, council charges and
contingency sit on top of that and we would like to see the total picture early rather than
find out later.

Our absolute ceiling including everything is **$950,000**. If the design cannot be built for
that we would rather know at concept stage than at tender.

The plunge pool is the item we would drop first if we have to. The bath in the first-floor
bathroom is the item we would drop last.

---

## 7. Programme

We want to be on site in **January 2027** and back in the house before the 2027 school year
finishes. We are renting nearby during construction, so every month on site costs us rent as
well as interest.

We understand a DA in a conservation area is not quick and we would rather lodge a good
application once than a fast one twice.

---

## 8. Constraints and things we already know

- **Heritage conservation area.** Anything visible from Georgina Street needs to be
  sympathetic. We have been told the first floor should be set back from the front wall. We
  are fine with that.
- **Party wall.** We are attached to No. 43 along the eastern boundary. Their addition went
  up in 2021 and we are on good terms with them, but we want the party wall handled properly
  and in writing.
- **Drainage.** The rear of the site holds water after heavy rain. The downpipes currently
  discharge to the surface. We assume this needs sorting out as part of the works.
- **Existing footings.** We do not know what the house is sitting on. If the first floor
  needs underpinning we would like to know before we commit to the design.
- **Access.** Georgina Street is narrow and there is no rear lane. Everything comes in and
  out the front. Council permits for skips and deliveries will be needed.
- **Neighbours.** No. 43 to the east, No. 39 to the west. We would like to talk to both
  before we lodge rather than let them find out through notification.
