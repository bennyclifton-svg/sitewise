## Background

Architectural services are sought for proposed multi-dwelling housing with Torrens subdivision across three lots totalling 3,240 m², comprising a feasibility target of 12 attached Class 1a dwellings—eight 3-bedroom and four 4-bedroom—over two storeys with no basement and private open space to each dwelling. [6]


The brief identifies a durable brick and fibre-cement envelope with repeatable wet-area and kitchen layouts. [6]


Early planning advice assumes an R3 Medium Density Residential scenario and a Council DA; the site-specific statutory position is not stated against current planning instruments, mapping and the planning certificate before lodgement. [7]

## Services and deliverables

Provide a concise return brief with the tender response, identifying amendments, qualifications, omissions and additional services.

1. Review the Client Development Brief, available architectural sections and shadow-diagram information; state the architectural scope, assumptions, exclusions, responsibility interfaces and client decisions required to progress the feasibility proposal. [6] 
2. Develop and document the architectural design for the proposed 12 attached Class 1a dwellings—eight 3-bedroom and four 4-bedroom—over two storeys with no basement, private open space, durable brick and fibre-cement envelope, and repeatable wet-area and kitchen layouts. [6] 
3. Coordinate the architectural design with the planner, civil, structural, building services, landscape, traffic, fire, façade, waterproofing, vertical-transport, security, signage and other appointed consultants; maintain coordinated drawings, schedules, design decisions and consultant-review responses for the three-lot, 3,240 m² site and proposed Torrens subdivision. [6] 
4. Undertake and document planning and design compliance work for the Council DA pathway, including confirmation of the current planning certificate, mapping and applicable instruments; respond to pre-DA matters and coordinate DA drawings, schedules, shadow studies and supporting consultant inputs. [7] 
5. Prepare the architectural documentation, specifications, finishes, joinery, glazing, waterproofing and external-works interfaces needed for tender and construction, with coordinated issue records and responses to tender queries; state the assumed tender and contract-administration pathway where not otherwise evidenced. [6] 
6. Provide construction-phase architectural services including review of contractor submissions and samples, site inspections, responses to RFIs, assessment of architectural variations, and coordination of design changes with the consultant team; maintain records of directions and unresolved design issues. [6] 
7. Undertake architectural completion inspections and coordinate defects, final drawings, warranties, product and finish information, and applicable completion or certification records for handover. [6]
