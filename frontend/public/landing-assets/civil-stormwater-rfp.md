## Background

The project concerns proposed multi-dwelling housing with Torrens subdivision across three lots with a combined area of 3,240 m²; the brief identifies attached Class 1a dwellings, subject to certification. [6]
Early planning advice assumes an R3 Medium Density Residential scenario and a Council DA, with the site-specific statutory position not stated before lodgement. [8]

## Services and deliverables

Provide a concise return brief with the tender response, identifying amendments, qualifications, omissions and additional services.

1. Review the site, survey/levels, architectural and consultant information, Council pre-DA feedback and applicable planning/authority requirements; establish the civil and stormwater design basis for the proposed multi-dwelling housing and Torrens subdivision across three lots (3,240 m²). [6] [7] 
2. Develop and coordinate civil concept, DA and construction documentation for site grading, bulk earthworks, retaining-wall interfaces, internal access/driveways, pavements, drainage and external civil works; clearly define design and interface responsibilities with architect, structural, hydraulic, landscape, traffic, geotechnical and utility consultants. [6] 
3. Undertake stormwater management design, calculations and supporting report(s), including detention, drainage, lawful point-of-discharge and water-quality/quantity measures as required by the evidenced approval pathway; review and either adopt, revise or supersede the existing below-ground OSD tank and outlet-detail information, including its structural and hydraulic interfaces. [3] 
4. Prepare the civil/stormwater drawings, specifications, notes, schedules and authority-submission material required for the selected DA pathway, including erosion and sediment control documentation and responses to civil engineering requests for information; state any approval-pathway assumption where not evidenced. [8] 
5. Coordinate the civil design with the current stormwater drainage-plan information and civil notes/legend, resolve clashes and documentation gaps, and issue coordinated revisions suitable for tender and construction. [4] [5] 
6. Provide tender and construction-phase services comprising responses to contractor queries, review of substitutions and shop/construction proposals within the civil/stormwater design responsibility, site inspections at appropriate hold points, and instructions or revised documentation required to maintain the approved design. 
7. Undertake discipline-appropriate completion services: inspect civil and stormwater works, review test records and as-built information, identify defects or non-conformances, and provide completion statements/certification only where authorised and within the consultant's statutory accreditation and agreed responsibility; exclude structural design of the OSD tank and hydraulic design responsibilities except for defined interfaces. [3]
