## Background

The proposed development is multi dwelling housing with Torrens subdivision across three lots totalling 3,240 m², comprising attached Class 1a dwellings subject to certification. [5]
The electrical services appointment is to support the project through its early Council DA pathway, for which the preliminary planning advice assumes an R3 Medium Density Residential scenario pending confirmation of the site-specific statutory position. [7]

## Services and deliverables

Provide a concise return brief with the tender response, identifying amendments, qualifications, omissions and additional services.

1. Review the Client Development Brief and available architectural, planning, utility and services information; establish the Electrical Services Engineer’s design basis, assumptions, exclusions and responsibility interfaces for the proposed multi-dwelling housing and Torrens subdivision. [5] 
2. Develop coordinated concept, DA and subsequent design documentation for electrical supply, maximum-demand assessment, metering strategy, main and dwelling distribution, submains, earthing, lighting and external/site electrical works for the proposed attached Class 1a dwellings, subject to certification and authority requirements. [5] 
3. Coordinate electrical provisions, spatial allowances, penetrations, containment and builder’s works with the architect, civil, structural, fire, hydraulic, mechanical, landscape, lighting, security, vertical-transport and utility consultants; maintain documented resolution of interfaces, design changes and outstanding information. [5] 
4. Investigate and document the applicable network connection and metering pathway, including utility applications, point-of-connection requirements, demand assumptions, authority inputs and any augmentation dependencies; state any pathway assumptions where confirmation is unavailable. 
5. Prepare staged electrical drawings, single-line diagrams, load calculations, equipment and luminaire schedules, specifications and tender/construction documentation proportionate to the confirmed design stage; coordinate tender queries, addenda and technical assessment of electrical submissions. 
6. Provide construction-phase design support, including review of contractor shop drawings, samples, substitutions and RFIs; inspect electrical works at appropriate stages and address non-conformances within the agreed design responsibility. 
7. Define and witness, where applicable, testing and commissioning of electrical, lighting-control, emergency-lighting/exit-sign and integrated security interfaces; obtain test records, certificates, as-built drawings, asset data, operating information and warranties, and issue the relevant completion statement or certification within the consultant’s professional scope. 
8. Identify services not included in the base appointment—such as specialist security design, communications/ICT design, photovoltaic systems, EV charging, emergency generation, authority fees and accredited-service-provider works—and state the proposed scope, approvals and interface responsibility for each if required.
