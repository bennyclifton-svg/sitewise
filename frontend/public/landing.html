Classes and destinations are SiteWise's own filing routes

One line out of forty pages.

The easement width off the title. The fill depth off the geotech. The yield the planner actually agreed to. SiteWise keeps the lines that carry the project and lets the rest go quiet.

Every line it keeps is marked with the file it came from, so spot-checking the ones that matter costs you a click, not an afternoon. That is the point of the citation — not auditing everything, but checking cheaply.

Then the facts meet each other.

A 1.5m drainage easement on the title. A footprint on the concept sketch. Separately, both are fine, and they sit in separate documents nobody reads side by side. Overlaid, one is sitting on the other — and you find out now, while it is still a sketch.
