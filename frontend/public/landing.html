Record the civil engineer’s appointment from the signed letter against their proposal and RFP. Note changes to scope, exclusions, fees and dates. Update the appointment, fee, programme and PMP. Flag discrepancies needing a decision.

Draft a civil works tender pack from current scope, drawings, specs and deliverables. Check revisions, interfaces and gaps. Research three local firms, showing suitability evidence and separating verified from unknown. Save the pack and align tender dates.

Compare the three builder tenders on the same scope. Reconcile exclusions, qualifications, provisional sums, alternatives and programme. Separate submitted prices from like-for-like adjustments; leave unsupported items unpriced. Explain whether the lowest remains preferable. Save the comparison and draft clarifications.

Find current drawings for the next package. Check revisions, superseded documents and conflicts. Prepare a transmittal with numbers, revisions, recipient and purpose. Save the draft and record outstanding coordination.

Process this month’s invoices against appointments, contracts and the cost plan. Check duplicates, previous claims, arithmetic errors and over-commitment. Allocate supported invoices, update the register and claimed-to-date, and show remaining commitments and forecast. Keep uncertain or disputed amounts visible.

Assess revised drawings, consultant advice and cost and time as one change. Trace affected scope, appointments, procurement and construction. Brief

The physical brief is a medium-density development of up to 12 attached, two-storey dwellings across three amalgamated lots, comprising eight 3-bedroom and four 4-bedroom dwellings at feasibility, private open space to each dwelling, and Torrens subdivision. The design is to provide a durable brick and fibre-cement envelope, repeatable kitchens and wet areas, and an accessible street path to one adaptable dwelling. There is no basement.

The scope includes demolition, clearance, bulk and detailed earthworks, retaining walls, foundations, superstructure, envelope, services, internal fitout, roads, parking, landscaping, drainage and stormwater. The design must resolve deep soil, tree canopy, waste storage and collection geometry, parking and traffic, and the proposed below-ground OSD interface before DA lodgement.

Acceptance for the next gate is a coordinated DA package supported by the required specialist evidence and a cost plan that states scope, allowances, contingency and exclusions. The 12-dwelling yield remains a feasibility target, not an approved outcome. Monthly owner reporting must connect scope, approvals, cost, programme and decisions, with named risk owners and evidence links.
