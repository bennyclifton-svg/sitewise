Five ways out of the chevron.

Every mark is judged the way it will actually be met: at header size beside the Satoshi wordmark, at favicon size, and on both grounds. Light panes use the proposed accessible blue #1a5fd0; dark panes keep #4285f4. The current mark leads as the baseline.

Why it needs replacing. The grey is #626A76, a neutral that appears nowhere else in the palette, so it goes muddy on ivory. Splitting a small mark across two colours fragments it: at 16px the blue half wins and the S never resolves. And the chevron gesture is the same one the page uses for its shutter fold, index bullets and scan datum, so the mark reads as more furniture rather than as an identity.

SiteWise
  • For: one weight, one colour, no fragmentation — it resolves at 16px. The 45° chamfers say "drawn with a set square", and it is literally the datum spine from the right edge of the landing page.
  • Against: it is a lettermark, and lettermarks are quiet. It will never be as memorable as a symbol.

B · Benchmark

Symbol

The surveyor's cut benchmark — a datum bar with a broad arrow beneath. A fixed reference point, which is the whole positioning.

SiteWise
  • For: real construction provenance, no SaaS competitor is using it, and it carries meaning — a benchmark is the fixed point everything else is measured from.
  • Against: bar-over-arrow risks reading as an upload glyph. The wide bar and the open, unfilled arrow are what hold it apart. Check the 16px column honestly.
